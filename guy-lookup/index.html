How to use
  1. Find: choose a family; search size, grade, supplier or part number. Accessories has a type filter.
  2. Compare: optionally filter nearby sizes or minimum rating. Compare one rating basis at a time.
  3. Inspect: select a model for key data and source/drawing; expand for more. Back to table keeps your filters.
  • Ratings: MBF/MBL = breaking force/load; WLL = working load limit; RHS = rated holding strength. MFL/tension rating keep manufacturer labels. Ratings, proof loads and efficiency are not design resistance; kN is a unit conversion.
  • Gaps: — means missing, not zero. Conflicts are withheld; E/EA is not inferred.
  • Before use: check edition, region, supplied product and installation. AU listing is not owner approval; similar size is not verified fit or assembly capacity. Temporary grips are not permanent mast terminations.
