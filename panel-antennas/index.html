Sources and limitations

Document status

  • Public PDF · review needed: original pages not yet checked; no mechanical values.
  • Catalogue / variant sheet needed: identity only; use Product page for the exact sheet. Active mechanics need a checked variant sheet.
  • Site record: identification from a released site document, not a manufacturer sheet.
  • Data conflicts: affected fields withheld; labels name the parameter or document. Details retain pre-release, preliminary and archived status; archived models aid existing-site review.

Size, mass and mounting

  • H: antenna height/length, excluding packaging.
  • Keep stated antenna/net/shipping/kit bases. Some CCI masses exclude RET; check it separately.
  • Pipe OD: only where published.

Wind forces and areas

  • Use published direction and speed; Max is a separate maximum-direction force.
  • EPA and equivalent flat plate area are distinct; neither comes from H × W. Conflicting areas stay unavailable.
  • Published force/survival ratings do not establish site loads or pole/mount capacity.

RF and Australian evidence

  • 4G / 5G uses documented AU site technology. Frequency alone establishes neither operator approval nor RF compatibility.
  • Gain, HPBW, PIM and power vary by frequency, array and beam mode; check the exact PDF.
  • Nokia/Ericsson active variants and legacy passive suffixes remain incomplete. CCI includes the earlier 252-code macro snapshot and superseded sheet, plus Small Cell / Outdoor DAS and Specialty.

Wind coefficient interpretation

Three categories: Published Cd, Derived Cd and Equivalent force coefficient.

Conditional estimates use C = F / (0.5 × ρ × V² × A), assuming ρ = 1.225 kg/m³ and nominal H × W front/rear or H × D side areas. These dimensionless comparison values are not manufacturer Cd.

Test density, true projected area, drag/resultant components and pole/bracket contribution remain unconfirmed. No maximum-direction coefficient, EPA/EFPA conversion or site-design coefficient is inferred; missing inputs stay unavailable.

Source dates and access

  • Per-record check dates are retained; latest intake does not mean older links were rechecked.
  • Links/revisions can change; PDF page jumps depend on the viewer.
  • Original documents are linked, not redistributed.
