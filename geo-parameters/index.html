Bearing basis and reference tablesAllowable pressure, ultimate screening and rock quality+

Allowable qa · a pressure limit with stated safety and settlement conditions. Ultimate qult · a bearing-failure limit, not a settlement check. Do not convert between them using a universal factor.

Soil bearing conditions and source modifiers

Look Table 21.4 gives presumed allowable bearing pressure for natural ground, based on B > 1 m and 25 mm settlement. B is footing width in metres. Sand assumes groundwater more than B below the base; halve the source value for saturated/submerged sand. This groundwater modifier is not applied automatically to clay.

The source divides by 1.2 for strip footings and reduces in ratio B for B < 1 m. No modifiers are calculated here. Gross/net basis is not stated in the soil table. The 25 mm basis is not a differential-settlement limit.

Sand, gravel and other ultimate-bearing cases need footing geometry, groundwater and a justified strength model; no generic ultimate range is inferred.

Deformation parameters and movement criteriaStiffness, compression and differences between supports+

Total settlement · movement of one support. Differential settlement · movement difference between supports. E and ν are analysis inputs, not settlement limits.

Differential movement and project criteria

Δs = |sA − sB|; L is the distance between supports. Δs/L indicates relative support rotation. Compare movements at the same time and separate overall tilt where relevant.

Acceptable movement depends on the structure and finishes. Include service loads, layer thickness, groundwater, stress history and construction sequence. The bearing table's 25 mm basis is not a differential-settlement limit.

Concrete–ground interface: μ and δConcrete–ground friction and interface angle+

Match the supporting material and contact condition. Interface δ differs from soil φ′.

Undrained adhesion, ca (kPa) needs a project interface value. GEC 6 discusses ca ≈ su as an idealisation; it is not adopted here. Do not add undrained adhesion to drained friction. No sliding resistance is calculated.

Pile shaft resistance — source factorsEstimation factors (dimensionless)+

Pile shaft · load transferred along the side. Pile base · load transferred at the toe. Anchor bond · load transferred over the fixed grout–ground length.

α and Ks tan δ below are dimensionless factors, not shaft-resistance values. Project shaft resistance fm,s and base pressure fb need installation, founding and compression/uplift conditions; no generic base resistance is inferred.

Before design · what is still needed from the project?
  • Ground profile, weak layers and groundwater
  • Loads, footing size, founding level and structural tolerance
  • Adopted strengths, bearing basis and settlement assessment
  • Sliding, uplift, overall stability and scour where relevant
  • Pile/anchor installation, group effects and verification testing
