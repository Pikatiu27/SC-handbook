# SC Handbook Publication Notice

## Status

SC Handbook is released as a **public beta engineering quick-reference handbook**. Build 0.7.92, updated 7 October 2026, adds eight band-specific historical Ericsson AIR 6488 records from the original manufacturer manual in a publicly released government filing. Panel Antenna Specs contains 1,543 model, family and order-code entries; 196 records have checked source sheets and 22 have dated Australian operator evidence. The eight new bands retain their own dimensions, unit weights, operating ranges and pole-installed wind forces from Rev M, 4 June 2019. The unsuffixed AIR 6488 site entry retains unavailable mechanical values because it does not identify the installed band or revision. Fifty-one explicitly printed Kaelus ordering codes remain searchable under qualified base records; independent variant mechanics are not inferred.

Sources are publicly accessible manufacturer publications, official regulator records and released site/planning documents. Based on Australian Communications and Media Authority information. Coverage is non-exhaustive. Historical originals, unstated revision dates and affected-field conflicts remain labelled; missing values remain unavailable. Australian site evidence for an unsuffixed model does not verify its installed revision or mechanical properties. Exact public Nokia AEQP/AQQY/AEQE, other AIR and P6BTEU02 manufacturer originals remain unverified. Incomplete originals whose restriction status cannot be established and internal/confidential marked publications were excluded. Catalogue inclusion establishes neither current availability nor Australian operator approval. Discontinued models and their replacements retain independent properties. Antenna, kit and shipping mass bases remain separate; installed totals with alternative kits are not subtracted to invent kit-only masses. Optical ports and active TX/RX paths are not RF antenna connector counts. Wind forces retain their original reference speeds; 42 m/s converts to 151.2 km/h, without rounding to 150. Ambiguous speeds suppress dependent coefficients. Conditional equivalent force coefficients are not manufacturer Cd or site design actions; the AIR-unit envelope does not receive an unsupported nominal-panel estimate. Original PDFs, private source archives and research/development evidence are excluded from this public artifact; source links lead to the public originals.

Panel remains under Product Lookup with Guy & Fittings; Section Properties remains in Steel Members. The accepted compact table, left-aligned folded Table guide and absence of routine PDF-check badges are retained. All other tools, including the 416-record Guy lookup, remain unchanged. Ground Parameters remains a For Review quick reference; independent engineering and manual accessibility acceptance remain deferred. Public availability does not promote Draft, For Review, Source_Not_Verified or Not evaluated results to issue-ready status.

## Engineering boundary

The handbook provides scoped calculations, product lookups and preliminary screening aids. It is not certified design software, a structural-analysis model, an issued design report or a compliance certificate. Users must verify the adopted Standard and edition, project inputs, actions, load paths, applicable limit states, product availability and all residual checks before design issue.

No page establishes that a complete component, connection, member, foundation or structure complies with all applicable requirements. The final engineer remains responsible for project adoption, design actions, detailing, constructability, inspection and issue approval.
