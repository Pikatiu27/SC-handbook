# SC Handbook Publication Notice

## Status

SC Handbook is released as a **public beta engineering quick-reference handbook**. Build 0.7.89, updated 7 October 2026, completes the reviewed public-source Panel data intake and Kaelus recheck. Panel Antenna Specs contains 1,534 model, family and order-code entries; 140 records have checked source sheets and 22 have dated Australian operator evidence. Kaelus has 37 entries, including P6BTEU02 from the official ACMA antenna register, two additional exact sector-antenna sheets and a dated P6RQLU01 manufacturer sheet recovered from a public government filing. Forty-seven explicitly listed Kaelus ordering codes are searchable under qualified base-model records; this does not verify independent variant mechanics or expand ordering templates.

Sources are publicly accessible manufacturer publications, official regulator records and released site/planning documents. Based on Australian Communications and Media Authority information. Coverage is non-exhaustive. Missing properties, older revisions and source conflicts remain labelled; catalogue inclusion establishes neither current availability nor Australian operator approval. Published wind forces retain their original speed and mass/kit bases. Conditional equivalent force coefficients use stated assumptions and are not manufacturer Cd or project wind actions. Original PDFs, the ACMA source archive and private development/evidence files are excluded from this public artifact; external links point to the public originals.

Panel remains under Product Lookup with Guy & Fittings; Section Properties remains in Steel Members. The accepted compact table, left-aligned folded Table guide and absence of routine PDF-check badges are retained. The existing 416-record Guy lookup, installation references and usage guide are unchanged. Ground Parameters remains a For Review quick reference; independent engineering and manual accessibility acceptance remain deferred. Public availability does not promote Draft, For Review, Source_Not_Verified or Not evaluated results to issue-ready status.

## Engineering boundary

The handbook provides scoped calculations, product lookups and preliminary screening aids. It is not certified design software, a structural-analysis model, an issued design report or a compliance certificate. Users must verify the adopted Standard and edition, project inputs, actions, load paths, applicable limit states, product availability and all residual checks before design issue.

No page establishes that a complete component, connection, member, foundation or structure complies with all applicable requirements. The final engineer remains responsible for project adoption, design actions, detailing, constructability, inspection and issue approval.
